# Codex Playbook · Memorize-This Version

> Just these. Remember them and you're ahead of 90% of people.

---

## 3-second version

**Codex = a programmer who speaks human language. You say what you want, it works. You're the boss.**

## Three iron rules

1. **Give full context** — Role + Task + Background + Acceptance
2. **One task at a time** — one thing per session
3. **Verify after use** — it's your employee, you're the QA

## Three habits

1. Create `AGENTS.md` in every project (write your rules)
2. Package frequently-used capabilities into Skills
3. Hand scheduled tasks to GitHub Actions

## Three phrases

- Speak plainly, skip jargon
- Correct it when unsatisfied: "this doesn't work, do it that way"
- It can't read your mind — say exactly what you want
